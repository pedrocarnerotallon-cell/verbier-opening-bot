Enjoy the Verbier 4Vallées ski area with friends or family, at the exceptional price of CHF 5.– a day.
On the programme: a wide range of entertainment, food and drink, and live music throughout the day.

⚠️ To qualify for the CHF 5.- fare, there are two conditions:

you must have reserved a place 
you must come dressed as Saint Nicholas

🚆 Special train to Le Châble
In partnership with RegionAlps, a free train will take you to the Opening Session in complete comfort. Departing from Geneva, it stops at the main towns along the Lake Geneva region all the way to Le Châble. Timetables will be published in November.

🎟️ Limited places: a limited number of tickets are on sale from Monday to Friday.

The Opening Session supports the Annapurna Association, based in Le Châble, in the Val de Bagnes. Founded in 2007, this Swiss humanitarian organisation works to support disadvantaged children in Pokhara, Nepal.
